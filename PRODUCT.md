# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

- **Primary: substation technician on site.** Example persona Jordan Reyes, Substation Technician II, 3 years in, covers 22 rural substations. Often dispatched at night or ahead of a storm, standing in front of energized legacy equipment, gloves on, one bar of signal, using a ruggedized tablet.
- **Secondary: dispatch / supervisor.** Needs to read at a glance whether the asset can stay in service and what the tech needs, without reading the conversation.
- **Evaluators:** hackathon judges watching a desktop demo of Scenario 1 (B-12 at Harlan Creek).

## Product Purpose

A voice-first field assistant that takes a technician from an alarm to an asset-specific answer during a real job. It pulls live site conditions (NWS weather and alerts, OpenStreetMap surroundings) to judge urgency and likely causes, answers from the utility's document library (manuals, bulletins, work orders, SCADA log, memos, retired techs' exit interviews), and at the end of the job writes a structured field report that becomes part of the knowledge base for the next tech. Success: the tech reaches for this before calling a retired colleague, and every completed job leaves the next tech better off.

## Positioning

The answer is drawn from the people and records that came before, specific to this asset and tonight's conditions, at the moment the job needs it, and each finished job adds to that knowledge rather than requiring anyone to "go write documentation".

## Operating Context

- Used on site, mostly hands-busy: speech in, speech out, with typing as fallback.
- Weak connectivity; cached API responses are acceptable during the demo.
- Shown on a laptop or projector for judging, and must also work on a field tablet.
- Scenario mode replays the cached 2027-01-19 winter storm snapshot for Scenario 1.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- React (Vite) frontend, FastAPI backend, Claude for reasoning, local Whisper for speech-to-text where the browser lacks speech recognition (Firefox).
- Sites: Harlan Creek (SUB-HCK) and Quarry Road (SUB-QRY). Assets in scope: HCK-OCB-B12, HCK-T1-LTC, QRY-F3-51.
- Out of scope: hiring, a new document repository, predictive maintenance / SCADA integration, enterprise API work.
- All companies, people, equipment and records are fictional; nothing is a real maintenance procedure.

## Brand Commitments

- Client name: Watt's Up Power Co. (fictional). Hackathon prompt "Lost Grid Knowledge" / "Knowledge at the Breaker", by METHOD.
- The user asked for a clean, professional dashboard look.

## Evidence on Hand

- `wattsup_document_library_extract/`: scenarios, asset records, work orders, SCADA log, cached weather, manuals, bulletins, procedures, memos, transcripts.
- Field reports generated by the app in `backend/knowledge_base/field_reports/`.
- No real customers, metrics or testimonials exist; do not invent any.

## Product Principles

1. Specific to this asset beats generic procedure.
2. Safety and honesty over confidence: say what is unknown and what must not be done.
3. Hands-free first; everything reachable by voice, nothing that needs precision tapping.
4. Conditions drive urgency: tonight's weather shapes the recommendation.
5. Every job leaves a record the next tech can use.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Large touch targets for gloved use, high legibility at night and in glare, full function by voice, and full function without voice (typing).
