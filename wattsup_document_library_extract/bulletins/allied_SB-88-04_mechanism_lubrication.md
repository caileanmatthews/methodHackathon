# Allied Switchgear Corp. — Service Bulletin SB-88-04
**Date:** March 1988 · **Applies to:** All BOC-series breakers with SM-3 and SM-4 mechanisms

## Subject: Mechanism Lubricant

Allied has received reports of field personnel substituting commercial greases on SM-series operating mechanisms.

**Use only Allied grease specification ASC G-11** on SM-series trip latches, rollers, and pivots. Substitution of other lubricants may affect mechanism timing and **voids the mechanism warranty.**

ASC G-11 is available from Allied parts distribution, part no. 88-1140 (1 lb can).

If mechanism timing is slow, check latch adjustment before re-lubricating. Do not add grease over existing grease.
