# Watt's Up Power Co. — Standard Maintenance Procedure GEN-PM-BRK-001
## Preventive Maintenance: Outdoor Power Circuit Breakers (All Types)
**Revision:** 6 · **Effective:** 2019-01-01 · **Owner:** Asset Management

### 1. Purpose
Provides the minimum preventive maintenance steps for all outdoor power circuit breakers 15 kV and above. Equipment-specific requirements are in the manufacturer's instruction book and take precedence.

### 2. Safety
Follow the Company Switching and Tagging Procedure and the Grounding Procedure. Discharge all stored energy before working on any operating mechanism.

### 3. Steps
1. Visual inspection: bushings, tanks, gaskets, oil level, cabinet condition.
2. Verify cabinet heaters operating.
3. Insulating medium test (oil dielectric or SF6 gas quality) per breaker type.
4. Contact resistance test, each pole.
5. Timing test (open, close) per manufacturer's acceptance values.
6. Trip and close coil checks.
7. Lubricate mechanism per manufacturer's instructions.
8. Record results in the maintenance system.

### 4. Intervals
Bulk oil breakers: 6 years. SF6 breakers: 8 years. Or per condition.
