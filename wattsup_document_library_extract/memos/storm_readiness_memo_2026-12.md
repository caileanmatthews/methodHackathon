# Watt's Up Power Co. — Ridge District Storm Readiness Memo
**Date:** 2026-12-01 · **From:** Ridge District Operations Manager

## Winter 2026–27 Storm Staffing

- On-call rotation for substation technicians is posted in the district office. Two techs on call per night from December 1 through March 15.
- When a Winter Storm Warning is issued for the district, system operations will perform pre-storm switching tests at priority substations, including Harlan Creek and Quarry Road.
- Mutual assistance crews are lodged at the county fairgrounds lot when activated.
- Reminder: fuel trucks to full at end of every shift during storm season.
- Storm meals are reimbursable per the Expense Policy; keep receipts.
