# Watt's Up Power Co. — Engineering Memorandum EM-2014-007
**Date:** 2014-03-10 · **From:** Substation Engineering · **To:** Ridge and Valley District Substation Maintenance

## Subject: Approved Substitute Lubricant for Allied SM-3 and SM-4 Breaker Mechanisms

**Background.** Allied Switchgear ceased operations in 1998. ASC G-11 grease is no longer available. Field reports (incl. WO-14-00287, Harlan Creek B-12) show aged G-11 hardening on trip latches and slowing trip times in cold weather.

**Decision.** Effective immediately, **Mobilgrease 28** is approved for Allied SM-3 and SM-4 trip latches, rollers, and pivot pins. This supersedes the lubricant requirement in Allied Service Bulletin SB-88-04 and in Allied instruction books.

**Requirements.**
1. Remove all existing grease with approved solvent before applying Mobilgrease 28. Do not mix greases.
2. Apply a thin film only.
3. Verify opening time and minimum trip voltage after lubrication, per the instruction book acceptance values.
4. Record the lubricant used on the work order.

Approved: R. Castellano, P.E., Manager, Substation Engineering
