# Watt's Up Power Co. — Fleet Vehicle and Mobile Device Policy (excerpt)
**Revision:** 2025-03

- Vehicles may not idle longer than 10 minutes except to power tools or maintain cab heat in temperatures below 32°F.
- Ruggedized tablets must be docked and charging when the vehicle is running.
- Tablets must have the current maintenance system app version installed. IT pushes updates on the first Tuesday of each month.
- Personal phones may be used for calls with other employees but **company data may not be stored on personal devices.**
- Report vehicle damage within 24 hours.
